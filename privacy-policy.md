# JobTrack Copilot — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

JobTrack Copilot is a Chrome extension that helps you manually save and organize job opportunities and track your application progress. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how it is used.

## 1. Information you enter

When you use JobTrack Copilot, you may choose to enter the following job-application information:

- Job title and company name
- Job location and job listing URL
- Employment type
- Application status
- Dates, including the date added, application date and follow-up date
- Notes you choose to write

The extension does not automatically collect job listings from LinkedIn or other websites. You enter the information yourself.

## 2. How information is stored

The information you enter is stored locally in your Chrome browser using the `chrome.storage.local` API. The current version does not operate a developer-owned server or account system for this information.

Your records remain associated with the local Chrome profile where you use the extension. They are not automatically synchronized to the developer or to a cloud account. Removing the extension, clearing browser data, or losing access to the Chrome profile may result in loss of locally stored records. You can use the built-in JSON backup feature to keep a copy.

## 3. How information is used

Your information is used only to provide the extension's core features:

- Display and organize the job applications you save
- Search and filter your saved applications
- Update application statuses and follow-up dates
- Export your records to a CSV file
- Create or restore a JSON backup at your request

## 4. Sharing and sale

The current version does not send your job-application records to the developer, sell them, or share them with third parties. CSV exports and JSON backups are generated locally when you request them. You decide where to save or share those files.

## 5. Permissions

JobTrack Copilot requests the Chrome `storage` permission so it can save and retrieve your records using Chrome's local extension storage. The current version does not request access to your browsing history, cookies, tabs, or LinkedIn page content.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your records remain in local extension storage until you delete them using the extension's controls, clear the relevant Chrome browser data, or uninstall the extension. You can delete individual records or use the dashboard's delete-all control. Export a backup first if you want to retain a copy.

## 7. Children's privacy

JobTrack Copilot is a general productivity tool for people managing job applications. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant product update. Material changes to data handling will be disclosed to users as required by applicable Chrome Web Store policies.
